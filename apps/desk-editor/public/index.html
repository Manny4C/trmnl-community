What went into this edition?

Newsflash covers a selected set of official sports, technology, AI, game, car, creator and space feeds. It is not a comprehensive world-news service. First-party announcements are credited as such. Interests filter the shared AI-reviewed pool; follow words narrow the title and feed description, rather than issuing an arbitrary AI prompt.

We strongly favor existing source images and official video thumbnails. Thumbnails may be designed preview graphics. We do not extract arbitrary frames, download videos, substitute unrelated stock photos, or present an illustration as a photograph of an event. This edition uses source-supplied visuals.

The AI editor’s activation and source status appear above. If an edit expires or a new story has not been reviewed, freshness-based fallback is labeled explicitly.

Newsflash is built for TRMNL: one visual story, a readable headline, and a clear reason to care. The AI runs off the device. Your TRMNL receives a compact story and image URL, then renders a still screen; it does not play video.
The always-on editor runs in scheduled cloud jobs, independently of your Mac. One shared ranking feeds all users, so a growing community does not multiply model calls. If the AI editor is unavailable, its freshness fallback is labeled. This first version uses existing source visuals.
Original headlines and supplied images remain credited to their sources. Image availability can change. Source previews retain their owners’ rights; our source code and layouts are available for the community. Built by Manuel, with support in the public repository.
