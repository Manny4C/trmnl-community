03 / A SIGNAL, NOT ANOTHER FEED

Your games.
Their next chapter.

Each row is a real developer announcement served by Steam. Patch and event labels come from words in the original title. A post about an event is not a promise that the event is happening tonight.

We check up to 24 recent posts per game, then sort matching posts newest first. Quiet games may have nothing in your chosen window. Source failures and cached posts are labeled; the radar never fills a gap with invented news.

“New” means a post ID wasn’t in this browser’s recent successful radar history. Each installed Recipe keeps its own bounded history; it does not sync with your browser or record whether you read an article.
