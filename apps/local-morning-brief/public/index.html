    THE PATCH WE COVER

    01 / WEATHER

    One forecast point.
    Clearly labeled.

    National Weather Service forecasts and active alerts for the selected city center. The large temperature belongs to the current forecast period; it is not a live observation. °F and °C use the same source. Source issue time and each successful check are shown.

    02 / GETTING AROUND

    A BART heads-up.
    Across the network.

    Up to two current agency advisories, including planned work. Notices are not filtered to your route and do not prove a delay is happening right now. Muni, AC Transit, traffic and train arrival times are not included. Check the official agency before traveling.

    03 / COMMUNITY

    Useful notices.
    From their source.

    Official BART community news for all three cities, plus SF Recreation & Park updates for San Francisco. These are agency announcements, not independent reporting or comprehensive city news. Dates from BART article URLs are labeled as dates, not invented publication times.

    Download the Recipe package ↓
    1. Create a private Polling plugin called Local Morning Brief with framework 3.4.3 or later.
    2. Copy the configuration fields, Shared and all four layouts from the ZIP.
    3. Use the polling URL below. A 30-minute refresh is suggested; TRMNL is not a live alert channel.
    Color has red and yellow accents. B&W preserves the same words, borders and hierarchy. Your existing playlist entries can stay in place.
    A brief with its sources attached.
    No account, API key, paid model or precise location is needed. We use only supported city centers. Standard hosting logs may contain the public city and display preferences in your URL. This browser stores your edition preferences locally; it does not share a home address.
    Sources are checked independently. A failed check is marked unavailable; it never becomes “no alerts.” Weather and news may use a dated, bounded warm-cache fallback. Weather alerts and transit advisories are never replayed as an outage fallback. Server caches are temporary, not durable storage. The display and its refresh schedule can lag; open official sources for current conditions.
    NWS open data · BART legacy API · BART data license · BART RSS · SF Rec & Park RSS
    BART data is provided as is and as available. BART retains its data rights and may change or stop the service. This independent project uses no official agency logos and is not endorsed by BART, NWS, SF Rec & Park or TRMNL. Article bodies and source photography are not reproduced.
