WHAT KEEPS THE STORY TOGETHER

Promises survive

A short recap does not erase a favor or a choice. Earned facts carry forward.

Small actions matter

Look, talk, help, repair, cross, rest or fight. A setback can still open the next step.

The world has limits

Messages need routes, people know only what they can learn, and a new week does not rewrite your party's past.
