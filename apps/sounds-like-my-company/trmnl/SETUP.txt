Sounds Like My Company — private plugin setup

1. Create a Private Plugin at https://trmnl.com/plugin_settings/new?keyname=private_plugin
2. Choose Polling, GET. Paste form-fields.yml into Form Fields. Save.
3. Use a public product description (10–300 characters), optional customers and keywords. Your company name is a label, not a website lookup. Fields are placed in a request URL; never enter confidential information.
4. Set polling URL to the single line below:
https://sounds-like-my-company.vercel.app/api/matches?name={{ company_name | url_encode }}&profile={{ profile | url_encode }}&audience={{ audience | url_encode }}&keywords={{ keywords | url_encode }}&exclude={{ exclude | url_encode }}&days={{ days }}&source={{ source }}&order={{ sort_order }}&count={{ result_count }}&format=trmnl
5. Refresh hourly or slower. There is no serverless transform or personal API key.
6. Paste shared.liquid into Shared. Paste the four matching .liquid files into Full, Half vertical, Half horizontal and Quadrant. Save every tab.
7. Preview your device type. Full shows up to your 3/6/8 setting, half shows two and quadrant one. Force a refresh and add to your playlist if needed.

Matching uses title words, explicit related-word groups and customer wording, not an LLM or verified competitor research. Must-have keywords are alternatives: any one must appear as a word. Exclusions remove any matching word. No-match results stay empty.
Coverage is bounded to Launch HN and Show HN. Up to two specific words from the public profile/keywords are sent to HN Search; at most 100 unique announcements are checked through official HN API per refresh. Show HN may be a project rather than a company. Dates are post dates. Older cached and partial-source editions are labeled. Cold outages return 503 instead of a fabricated edition.
