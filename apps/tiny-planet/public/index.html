02 / THE PLAN IS SMALL. THE WORLD IS SHARED.

Make room for
something good.

01

Grow the island.

Plant on a meadow for 1 seed. Water a sapling for 1 water; it becomes a grove and gives 2 seeds + 1 wood. Gather 2 water from the sea, 1 fallen wood from a grove, or 1 stone from a rock.

02

Find the next horizon.

A beacon costs 6 wood + 4 stone on a meadow. Once the island has 8 groves, select its beacon and Sail. We all arrive at a fresh island together. Need room? Clear a sapling, grove or beacon to make a meadow. A beacon returns 2 wood + 1 stone.

03

One owner. One move.

One successful move per verified TRMNL account per UTC day, across all devices and sign-ins. There is no daily cap on total community moves. Invalid moves do not spend your allowance.

Someone always tends the planet.

A labeled Owner autopilot is scheduled every calendar day, including weekends. It waters, plants, gathers, builds and sails toward the shared goal. Its one daily system move is separate from each player’s allowance. Hourly recovery checks avoid duplicates; outages and late catch-up are visible in the history.

Manual Recipe setup

Create a Polling private plugin with framework 3.4.3 or later. Copy Shared and all four layouts from the archive. Refresh every 30 minutes or slower. No API key or personal field is sent in the polling URL.

A quiet game.
A clear agreement.

Connect with TRMNL requests account/profile and device read access solely to verify an account ID and at least one device. We never change your TRMNL settings, ask for an API key, or show your real name. Authorization tokens are encrypted on the server, expire within two hours and are deleted when you disconnect. The game retains a hashed account identity to enforce the daily allowance. Public history shows only “Player” or “Owner autopilot,” move coordinates and times.

You can revoke access in TRMNL’s Connected agents. A fresh ownership check is required before every move; outages fail closed. Expired sessions need a new connection. Free service quotas may pause play; no paid service or AI model is needed for this game. Contact us through Support for data removal. Removing an identity does not erase its daily quota immediately.

This is an independent community project, not affiliated with TRMNL. All art, rules and code are original. We store game actions, not your device’s friendly ID, address, email or API key.
