No API key, real name, email or public player profile is required. The shared Tiny Planet integration requests read/profile access and only reads account/device endpoints for ownership verification. Tokens are encrypted in our existing personal PostgreSQL service; canvas sessions last at most two hours and end at midnight UTC. Ownership is checked again before every published batch. Failures stop edits; a QR/code alone is not ownership proof.

Daily quotas retain a hashed account identifier. Public history shows edit counts and timestamps, never your account ID. Pixel changes and their audit history are stored on our US-hosted personal service. Standard hosting logs may include public entry links. Draft retry data stays in this tab’s session storage. Revoke the integration in TRMNL’s Connected agents or contact support for deletion. Removing an identity does not erase an already-used allowance immediately.

No paid model or new paid service is used. Free hosting/database quotas may affect availability. This is an independent community project, not affiliated with TRMNL. Report content or problems through support.
